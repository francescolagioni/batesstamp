Privacy Policy

BatesStamp does not collect, transmit, or store any data about you or your documents.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no personal data, no usage data, no diagnostic data, no crash reporting, and no analytics of any kind. No information about you, your Mac, or the documents you process is gathered.

What the app transmits

Nothing. BatesStamp works entirely offline. It is distributed as a sandboxed application without the network entitlement, which means the operating system itself prevents it from making a network connection. This is not a statement of intent; it is a property of the app that can be inspected.

Your PDFs are read from the files and folders you choose, and the stamped copies are written to the output folder you choose. They go nowhere else. There is no cloud service, no upload, no sync.

Accounts

There are none. BatesStamp has no sign-up, no login, no licence server and no activation. It is bought once through the Mac App Store and it runs.

What the app stores on your Mac

Two things, both local, both only so the app is useful the next time you open it:

Your settings
Prefix, suffix, digits, position, font, size and colour, plus the last Bates number used for each prefix so the app can offer to continue a production where it left off. Held in the app's own preferences, on your Mac.
A reference to your last output folder
A security-scoped bookmark, so the folder chooser opens where you were last time. It grants access to that folder only, and only to this app.

Passwords for protected PDFs are held in memory for the length of the batch and are never written anywhere.

Third parties

There are none. BatesStamp uses no third-party frameworks, no software development kits, no advertising, and no external services. Nothing is shared with anyone, because nothing is collected in the first place.

Children

The app is a professional document tool and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page. An app update that started collecting data would be a different app, and would say so plainly here first.
